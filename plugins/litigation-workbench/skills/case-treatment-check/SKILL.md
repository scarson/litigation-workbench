---
name: case-treatment-check
description: Check whether a U.S. case can still support a particular proposition, investigate adverse history and later treatment, and prepare or reconcile counsel's dated Shepard's check. Use for good-law, Shepardize, and update-my-authorities requests.
---

Start with the cited case, the exact proposition or draft sentence, the forum and the requested as-of date. For a bare citation, resolve the case and report any direct history you can substantiate. Ask which proposition and forum matter before offering a reliance recommendation. Resolve short forms to their antecedents. Give separate rows for materially different propositions drawn from the same opinion.

1. Read the cited opinion at the pinpoint. Identify the court, relevant opinion or vote, holding and fit. Check the case's own subsequent history (including rehearing, amendment, vacatur and reversal) separately from its treatment in other cases. For a vacatur or amendment, read the order and identify which judgment or version it affects.
2. Use the sources and tools actually available and permitted for the matter. With authorized public research, start with the bare citation. If Descrybe Legal Engine is connected, use its case-status and citing-case tools. With CourtListener, look up the case and its citing opinions. Public court sites and supplied opinions also work. Search for controlling later decisions, limits or distinctions, and material changes to statutory text or rules where relevant. For each material result, open the deciding court’s opinion or order and give the later citation, date, pinpoint and link. Check the voice, hierarchy, forum and exact point affected. A dissent, a party’s argument and an out-of-circuit criticism carry different weight from a controlling holding. A result count, automated label, search snippet or missing hit supplies no affirmative clearance.
3. For a Shepard’s check, give counsel a compact queue keyed to the propositions. Counsel opens the exact case in firm Lexis, selects Shepardize, inspects appellate history and adverse citing decisions, and reads the opinions behind material signals. Ask counsel to return their own authorized note of the service, reviewer, check date, relevant decision and pinpoint, and effect on the proposition. Keep credentials, reports, headnotes and exports in the firm's licensed environment. Attribute any completed check to the person who performed it. If another service was used, name it accurately.
4. Lead with any proposition counsel should withdraw, narrow or re-source and say why. Return a row per material proposition with the original pinpoint, later primary-source finding, effect in the forum, and **licensed check status**. Use “pending” when there is no sufficient note, “counsel checked [service] on [date]” when the note covers that point and cutoff, or “update needed” with the missing date range, unresolved conflict or mismatch. Preserve an older check as dated history. In a historical assignment separate developments after the cutoff. If there is no public search access, identify exactly which supplied sources you read and put the remaining work in the queue.

Affirmative adverse primary text is actionable even with Shepard’s pending. Describe the demonstrated defect and suggest a replacement or narrower use supported by the record. If a negative decision addresses another issue, explain its scope and leave independent treatment of the proposed point open as appropriate. Report the search scope and date and distinguish a provisional public-source read from counsel’s dated citator review.
