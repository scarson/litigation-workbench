---
name: lexis-research-handoff
description: Turn a legal issue into a research assignment for a lawyer to run in Lexis and synthesize independently obtained authorities and counsel's verification notes.
---

For a new assignment, identify forum, governing law, posture, issue and as-of date. Ask for any missing item that would change the search, or provide labeled branches. Reduce the question to the facts that can change the rule. Draft search text that counsel can enter in Lexis: a plain-language formulation, targeted terms and connectors, and a search for adverse authority or exceptions. Suggest filters and what would distinguish a promising result. Leave case names blank until supported by a source.

Counsel operates Lexis and Shepard's under the firm's license. This workflow needs no login, browser control, API or connector. Use independently obtained court opinions, statutes, rules or counsel's own authorized analysis for synthesis. Do not request Lexis downloads, editorial content or citator reports for an external AI account. If the question turns on a rule or order, identify the issuing court, operative version and effective date to check. For a filing procedure, include any relevant local rule, judge's standing order and case-specific order for counsel to obtain.

For each authority returned, distinguish what the primary text shows from what counsel reports checking. Record citation, court/date, pinpoint, proposition, posture, weight in the specified forum, adverse or limiting language, source and treatment-check date. Mark missing text or a missing treatment check explicitly. Do not infer current validity from a citation match, a link, an old signal or absence of negative results. If counsel supplies only a research question, stop with a usable research plan.
