---
name: damages-reconciliation
description: Reconcile amounts claimed in a business dispute with contracts, invoices, ledger entries, payments and evidence of performance.
---

Set the as-of date, currency and scope. Identify the transaction grain, match invoices to receipts and credits by ID, and distinguish reversals, duplicate copies and unapplied transactions. Treat blank, missing and zero as different values. Flag partial periods and unexplained opening balances.

Create a bridge from the amount demanded to the book balance and to amounts supported under each stated assumption. For each disputed component, show the contractual trigger, record of performance, payment or credit, evidence on the other side, and the source pinpoint. Keep a claimed offset or estimate in its own row until the assumptions support applying it. Distinguish an unsupported trigger from evidence that the event never occurred.

When a ledger comes from a PDF table, check column alignment, signs, carried subtotals and the total against the source page before using extracted amounts. For a time-based payment trigger, show the event, any required signatures, the counting convention, and whether the resulting date falls within the cutoff. Keep the as-of date fixed when comparing the same account before and after a later production.

Reconcile the arithmetic a second way and cite the document section or ledger entry for each amount. If counsel requests a workbook, separate inputs, assumptions and calculations, use formulas and include a tie-out to an independently derived figure. For a narrative request, give the bridge in the answer. Avoid conclusions about legal recoverability or interest without counsel's specified basis. Report unsolved differences, even when small.
