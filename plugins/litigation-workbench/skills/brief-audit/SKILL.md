---
name: brief-audit
description: Find where a draft paragraph overstates a supplied exhibit or primary opinion. Prioritize consequential defects, show the limiting passage and give the narrowest supportable rewrite.
---

Use the draft as a list of claims to test. Identify its version, the assigned issue, posture and available sources. Break compound sentences into their material factual and legal propositions. For each challenged proposition give the draft paragraph, assertion, cited source and pinpoint, what the source actually supports, the strongest contrary passage and the practical effect.

Check quoted text in context, including omissions, qualifications and the speaker. For a case, distinguish the holding from dicta, dissent, party argument and a court's description of facts. Check court, posture and factual fit. Use only authorized primary law and attorney-provided treatment notes. Separate missing material and unverified treatment from actual contradiction.

Track three distinct questions for a material citation: Did you identify the right opinion? Does the cited passage support this exact proposition? Who checked subsequent treatment, and when? For “Id.” or a short form, trace the reference to its antecedent. Leave ambiguous matches unresolved. Use reporter pages or numbered opinion paragraphs where available. With authorized public research, look up the bare public citation first and read the relevant opinion. Keep draft text and matter details out of external queries unless counsel has approved that use.

Lead with the defects most likely to change counsel's argument, then list the remaining exceptions and the scope covered. Suggest the narrowest supportable rewrite or the missing research. Identify any draft section you could not review. When a redline is requested, retain the original and return a separately named revision with locatable edits. Label the status of any citation you have not independently read.

When counsel asks whether cases are still good law, use case-treatment-check for the identified propositions if available. Carry its later-opinion pinpoints and dated verification status back to the affected draft paragraphs.
