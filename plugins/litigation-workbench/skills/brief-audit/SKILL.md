---
name: brief-audit
description: Audit a draft brief or opponent's argument against the supplied record and primary authorities and return prioritized corrections.
---

Use the draft as a list of claims to test. Identify its version, the assigned issue, posture and available sources. Break compound sentences into their material factual and legal propositions. For each challenged proposition give the draft paragraph, assertion, cited source and pinpoint, what the source actually supports, the strongest contrary passage and the practical effect.

Check quoted text in context, including omissions, qualifications and the speaker. For a case, distinguish the holding from dicta, dissent, party argument and a court's description of facts. Check court, posture and factual fit. Use only authorized primary law and attorney-provided treatment notes. Separate missing material and unverified treatment from actual contradiction.

Lead with the three defects most likely to change counsel's argument, then list the remaining exceptions and the scope covered. Suggest the narrowest supportable rewrite or the missing research. Identify any draft section you could not review. When a redline is requested, retain the original and return a separately named revision with locatable edits. Label the status of any citation you have not independently read.
