Start here for your first matter

  1. Choose one disputed fact. Open a fresh Work or Codex project for the matter. Add two or three documents that bear on the fact, such as a contract, an email and a demand. Use public or fictional material on a personal account until your firm clears it for the matter.
  2. Make one file you can use. Select Litigation Workbench, replace the brackets below, and send the prompt.
    Use evidence-matrix. We represent [party]. I need to decide [question]. Using only the attached record, make a one-page proof matrix I can edit. For each material proposition, give the strongest evidence on each side, the document and pinpoint, and the missing proof that could change my decision.
  3. Check the row that matters most. Open the cited exhibit at the pinpoint. If a legal rule drives your decision, use CourtListener to retrieve the public opinion. Give LegalQuants Litigation your proposed sentence and the opinion, ask whether the passage supports it, then check current treatment in Shepard’s at work.
