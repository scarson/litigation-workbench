Fictional examination · Job export

Counsel represents a printer disputing an invoice for rework. The foreperson is an individual witness. Counsel wants to establish the sequence and who, if anyone, authorized the rework ticket to be closed.

E1 · Service desk log, p. 1, rows 12–13. Ticket 411, “job export jam.” Opened Wednesday at 10:02. Closed Wednesday at 10:44. Both entries use the same local clock.
E2 · Foreperson interview, p. 2, lines 5–8. “I restarted the job manually that morning. I don't remember the time. I saw a finished stack in the tray when I came back from lunch. The service desk handles ticket closure. Some tickets require supervisor approval.”
E3 · System guide, p. 3, paragraph 2. A ticket can be closed when referred to another team or when a problem is resolved. The log does not record the press finish time or an approver.

Outline I
Objective: establish when the printer finished and explore who authorized closure.
1. “Why did you restart the job after it had finished printing?” [E1, rows 12–13; E2, lines 5–8.] If disputed, ask what the foreperson personally observed at the press and when.
2. “Do you know whether supervisor approval was required to close ticket 411?” [E2, lines 5–8; E3, paragraph 2.] If the witness knows whether it was required, ask “Who approved this closure?”
3. “The ticket was opened at 10:02 and closed at 10:44 by the same local clock, correct?” [E1, rows 12–13.] If the witness cannot authenticate the entries, ask who maintains the service log.

Outline II
Objective: establish what the record dates and the basis for any closure authority.
1. “What do you personally recall about when the press finished in relation to your manual restart, if you can place the two?” [E1, rows 12–13; E2, lines 5–8; E3, paragraph 2.] If the witness can place them, ask what observation fixes their order.
2. “Do you know whether anyone actually approved closing ticket 411?” [E2, lines 5–8; E3, paragraph 2.] If the witness recalls a specific approval, ask who gave it and how the witness personally learned of it.
3. “The ticket was opened at 10:02 and closed at 10:44 by the same local clock, correct?” [E1, rows 12–13.] If the witness cannot authenticate the entries, ask who maintains the service log.
