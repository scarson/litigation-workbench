Fictional record · Peeling finish

The buyer claims inadequate curing caused two lots of coated clips to peel. Examine the supplier's process technician, Oren Vale, about what the run records could establish. Two furnace cycles, K-17 and K-19, were performed on different racks.

T1 · Process sheet, p. 2, acceptance note. “Part metal at 170 °C or higher for ten continuous minutes.” The note sets a part-metal requirement. An oven setpoint is a command to the furnace.
T2 · Recovery index, p. 1, rows 17 and 19. Both K-17 and K-19 are marked “controller archive retained.” The date in this index is the date the archive was copied to the recovery server.
T3 · Vale interview, p. 3, lines 4–9. “I programmed both cycles. On those controls a load probe can be attached to a clip. The historian can keep its signal if the run is configured for it. Programming the oven and attaching the load probe are separate jobs. I didn't unpack either retained archive.”
T4 · Restoration inventory, p. 6, entry 19, signed by the recovery analyst. “Verified inside the retained K-19 package: time-stamped PROGRAM.SET and PART.METAL series. Channel card identifies PART.METAL as the clip-mounted load probe.” No K-17 channel inventory is supplied.

Outline
Objective: establish what Vale programmed and what an archived temperature record could resolve about the buyer's curing theory.
1. You programmed both of these cycles? [T3, p. 3, lines 4–9.] If Vale qualifies, ask which part of the programming Vale personally performed, if any.
2. What does the acceptance note require the metal itself to do? [T1, p. 2.] If Vale describes an oven setting, ask what, if anything, links that setting to the temperature of a clip.
3. Did you inspect either retained package? [T3, p. 3, lines 4–9.] If yes, ask which package and what Vale personally saw. If no, move to the recovery analyst for file contents.
Counsel note on next proof: Obtain the retained [[run]] archive from the recovery analyst. Compare its part-metal series to the ten-minute requirement. That comparison will test the cure theory for the monitored clip. [T1, p. 2; T2, p. 1, rows 17 and 19; T4, p. 6, entry 19.]

Outline variants: substitute K-17 or K-19 for [[run]].
