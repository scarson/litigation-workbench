# Can the buyer still inspect on Friday?

Fictional internal preparation note for a Thursday negotiation. The buyer wants to complete its inspection and preserve its contractual rights. The record contains two contract clauses and two emails. No governing law is supplied.

## Original

The seller agreed to extend the contractual inspection deadline through Friday.

## Steelman before independent review

Before the Wednesday cutoff, the buyer put its request for Friday in writing, and the seller’s project manager said Friday worked for scheduling. The exchange gives the buyer a concrete basis to insist that Friday remains available for inspection.

The steelman replaces a claim about the seller’s agreement with the two acts we can document. The word “insist” is the part counsel still needs to test.

## Reviewer’s return

> **R1 — “Friday remains available for inspection.”** The seller can keep the dock open after the buyer’s contractual rights expire. M2 promises access. Section 9 directs notices to the designated inbox, and M1 went only to the project manager. The rewrite corrects the original’s claim that the seller agreed to an extension, but “insist” still blurs access and preservation of rights.
>
> **R2 — “put its request for Friday in writing.”** Section 4 gives the buyer a unilateral extension by timely notice. The buyer’s best answer is that M1 identified Friday before 5 p.m. and M2 acknowledged it. “Can we have through Friday?” also reads as a request for permission. Proof of timely delivery to the designated inbox would address delivery. Whether actual receipt, agency, or waiver cures a defect requires governing-law research.
>
> For Thursday’s call, rely on the scheduling commitment alone. Seek a signed agreement from an authorized seller representative expressly preserving or reinstating the buyer’s inspection rights through Friday.
