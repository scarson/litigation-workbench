Five dimensions, each 0–2. (1) Client decision and adverse case: tests both the client's decision-critical point and the strongest competing reading. (2) Source fidelity: supported factual premises and usable pinpoints, including in branches and notes. Verify any asserted relationship between facts. (3) Witness foundation: uses personal knowledge, distinguishes actors, and develops the limits of recollection or authority. (4) Next proof: identifies a particular obtainable source, its likely owner or route, and what it would resolve. (5) Live use: five distinct lead questions for twelve minutes, in a usable sequence, with a conditional branch that changes what counsel can learn. For each dimension award 2 if met, 1 if partially met or requiring a material edit, 0 if missing or materially false. Do not award extra credit for length or quoting the key. Equally functional alternatives count. For each below-full score, cite the specific question or omission. Flag any material factual premise counsel must repair before asking; a neutral question, properly supported condition, minor style issue, or an unpursued issue alone is not a flag.
