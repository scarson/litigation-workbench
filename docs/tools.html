What should I leave enabled?

After the session, leave Workbench installed and switch it off. Start daily work with one general litigation bundle. For a short, exhibit-based fact-witness examination, try LegalQuants Litigation first. Its outline turned a custodian’s name into a concrete records request in our blind comparison. For a claim chart or a briefing from an existing matter log, try Claude for Legal. Keep CourtListener available for public sources and use the firm’s Shepard’s workflow in Lexis. Grade the three anonymous deposition outlines yourself.

Bring Workbench back for a particular output you value. Examples are a damages bridge from invoices to contractual payment triggers, the original argument beside a steelman and an independent reviewer’s objections, or a proposition-by-proposition handoff for your own Shepard’s checks. Make it a daily default only if a recurring job takes less time including your corrections. The deposition comparison covered four questions on one fictional record.

Compare two tools on your own question

  1. Install the bundles you want to try. For an ordinary-language test, enable one competing bundle at a time and begin a fresh chat for each run. Give each the same approved exhibits, question and output format. Keep the first answer and your corrections out of the second chat.
  2. For a deposition comparison, try the short assignment below. Open both outlines beside the exhibit. Which one asks a question the witness can answer, preserves the qualification and gives you a useful follow-up? Record how many minutes it takes to get each outline ready to use.
  3. Keep your preferred bundle enabled for everyday requests. For an occasional second opinion, re-enable the other bundle, start a fresh chat, explicitly select its skill and give it the same record. A switch applies to the whole bundle, including its other skills.
We represent the buyer and will examine the seller’s project manager. On page 2 of Exhibit M1 the manager wrote, “I haven’t seen the signed acceptance.” Draft four questions about what she personally knew. Include foundation, an exhibit pinpoint, and a follow-up if she says someone else handled acceptance.

What to score. An outline loses the point if it turns “I haven’t seen it” into “no one signed it.” Give credit for a question that identifies who would know or where the signed copy would be kept.

  • ChatGPT Work or desktop. Type @ in the composer and select the bundled skill when it appears. In a Codex view with Sources → Use plugins, select the installed plugin and name the skill in your request. Check the publisher when names look alike. In a managed workspace, use the chat picker to compare tools without changing workspace-wide settings.

Which ordinary requests overlap?

Codex can use skill descriptions to select a workflow. The words below are examples of requests a lawyer could actually type. Adding the desired work product often resolves the ambiguity.

“Help me prepare for this deposition.”
Three skills fit. For admissions, exhibit foundation and a follow-up for each denial, try Workbench record-to-deposition. For an outline tied to case theory and witness documents, try Claude deposition-prep. For corporate designee topics, witness preparation or work with the transcript afterward, try LegalQuants depositions.
“Organize the evidence.”
Ask Claude for a dated timeline (chronology) or a chart for specified claim or defense elements (claim-chart). Ask Workbench for factual propositions with stable IDs, support, contrary evidence and missing proof (evidence-matrix). Ask LegalQuants to inventory a production against requests (docreview) or organize an accepted matter’s workspace (organize-case-docs).
“Check my brief.”
Ask LegalQuants cite-check to check the filing’s citations and quotations. Ask Workbench brief-audit for the paragraph that outruns its source and a defensible rewrite. For new prose, Claude brief-section-drafter and LegalQuants writing both draft. Give them the same section if you are comparing style.
“Pressure-test this.”
Workbench argument-review keeps the original, steelman, independent challenge and decisions on revisions. For LegalQuants’ check of internal logic, dates and figures, explicitly pick pressuretest. Its publisher marks it explicit-only in Codex, even though its description uses the words “pressure-test.”
“Check this case.”
Ask CourtListener for the public opinion or filing. Ask Descrybe for a later-treatment search if connected. For the effect on one sentence and a dated firm citator check, ask Workbench for case-treatment-check. For searches you will run yourself on an open issue, request a Lexis search plan. Retrieval and treatment review can be successive steps on one assignment.
“Open this matter” or “update the client.”
Claude matter-intake starts its matter log. LegalQuants new-matter starts its controlled intake. Choose the one whose record you intend to maintain. For a call from the existing log, use Claude matter-briefing. For a recipient-ready draft, ask LegalQuants for client-update. Pick the same home for later updates to avoid maintaining two matter logs.
“Help with this demand, subpoena or privilege log.”
Claude has dedicated demand-received, demand-draft, subpoena-triage and privilege-log-review workflows. LegalQuants correspondence covers demand and response letters, document-discovery covers objections and conferral, and docreview can build a privilege review queue. Specify the artifact you need and the source set it should cover.

Claude for Legal (compatible with Codex)

The setup page installs Litigation Legal from Anthropic’s Claude for Legal collection. It gives Codex 19 named skills. A skill is a set of instructions for a particular job. For example, claim-chart tells Codex how to map evidence to each element, while deposition-prep builds a witness outline around the issues and exhibits. You can start either with approved local files.

Try a civil claim chart in a fresh Codex chat

  1. Install the litigation plugin. In a fresh local Codex chat, select Litigation Legal in the plugin picker and ask for claim-chart.
  2. On your own matter, give it your side, the elements you want to test, and the exhibits it may use. Specify a matter folder or ask for the chart in chat. To try it immediately, copy the fictional record below.
Use claim-chart in civil mode. We represent the buyer. For this exercise the only stipulated issue is timely written demand. Agreement A §5 requires the buyer to send written demand to the seller by Wednesday at 5 p.m. M1 is the buyer’s internal draft demand, saved Wednesday at 3 p.m. These are the only materials. Put one row in chat with the evidence, pinpoint, contrary evidence, and gap. What single record would most change your assessment?

Read the row. M1 establishes that someone prepared a demand before 5. The time, recipient and content of any transmission remain open. A good chart asks for the sent message or delivery record. If the row says the buyer missed the deadline, ask which exhibit proves that.

Run it again. Add fictional M2, a sent email showing the buyer sent that demand to the seller at 4:42 p.m. Wednesday. Ask for the old and new row side by side. The revised finding about dispatch should cite M2. Then ask deposition-prep for the questions that would authenticate M2 and identify its attachment.

Give it the witness, theory and exhibits to get an examination outline with potential impeachment material.

privilege-log-review
Screen an approved log for entries that need a closer look. Have counsel resolve the privilege calls against the documents.

Handle demands, subpoenas and preservation

demand-received
Turn an incoming demand into a triage note with response options.
demand-intake
Collect facts, leverage, objectives and open questions before writing an outgoing demand.
demand-draft
Turn that intake into a draft letter and review checklist.
subpoena-triage
Review the subpoena and service facts for scope, burden, potential objections and dates counsel should confirm.
legal-hold
Prepare a hold notice, refresh or release for counsel’s review, or report the status of recorded holds.

Keep a matter and its reporting current

matter-workspace
Create or switch the active matter workspace before working with a different client’s record.
matter-intake
Open a matter record with parties, issues, owners and key dates.

matter-close
Capture the outcome and archive the matter from the active portfolio.

Litigation Workbench

Seven assignments turn a small matter record into something you can edit. Choose a proof matrix, deposition outline, damages reconciliation, Lexis research handoff, brief audit, case-treatment check or argument review. The argument review preserves your draft and a separate steelman, then tracks an independent reviewer’s objections into the revision when delegation is available.

Try the damages reconciliation. Open a fresh chat with Workbench selected. Supply a ledger and the provisions that make each payment due, or try these fictional figures.

Use damages-reconciliation. Invoice A is $25,000. The first $20,000 is due. Receipt B applies $10,000 to that portion. Contract §4 makes the last $5,000 due only upon a signed acceptance certificate. The record contains the invoice, receipt and contract. Give me the open ledger balance, the amount supported as due, and the document I should request next.

Check the bridge. The ledger has $15,000 open. The record supports $10,000 as due and leaves the $5,000 trigger unresolved. Ask which line would change if a signed certificate arrived. All seven assignments and prompts · Worked argument review · Fictional matter files.

LegalQuants Litigation

A practitioner-built collection for reviewing and drafting litigation work. Use cite-check for a brief and its sources or depositions for witness preparation. For an opponent’s best response, explicitly select pressuretest in the skill picker. The bundle also covers discovery, document review, client updates and correspondence.

Try a cite check. Select the plugin in a fresh chat. Attach your numbered draft and the cited opinions it may read. The fictional practice file includes a two-sentence draft and a public opinion for this comparison.

Use LegalQuants Litigation’s cite-check on the attached draft and opinions. For each challenged sentence, give the paragraph number, the proposition, the controlling passage and reporter page. Identify any authority you could not read. Show me the most consequential correction first.

Check the correction. Open the page and read the sentences on either side. Check whether the majority or a separate opinion spoke. If a real citation only supports a narrower proposition, ask for a revised sentence you can use. Try the two-sentence audit · Install LegalQuants Litigation · Publisher’s skill directory.

CourtListener

Free Law Project connects the chat to public opinions and available RECAP docket filings. Use it when you need the source document. Start with a citation or a court and docket number. The hosted connector uses a CourtListener account.

Use CourtListener to retrieve 477 U.S. 317. Return the case name, court, date and opinion link. Find the majority’s discussion of the movant’s initial burden at reporter page 323. Give me the passage and identify a source copy where I can check the printed page.

Check the locator. Open the opinion and find the printed 323. For a docket request, match the court, docket number and entry before opening the filing. If the entry has no available document, record that gap. Install CourtListener · Free Law Project’s tools and account guide.

Descrybe Legal Engine

This optional subscription adds in-chat citation resolution, quotation checks and searches for later treatment. Connect an authorized Descrybe account. Give it a public citation, the precise proposition, forum and as-of date. A later decision that limits a different proposition may leave yours intact.

Use Descrybe to check [citation and pinpoint] for [precise proposition] in [court] as of [date]. Retrieve the later decision that most affects that proposition. Give me its court, date, pinpoint and source link. Explain which words in my proposed sentence I should reconsider and what coverage remains open.

Read the later court’s words. Confirm what it limited or rejected and whether that reaches your proposition. Record unresolved coverage even if the search returns no adverse result. Install Descrybe · Provider’s tools and access guide.

Lexis and Shepard’s at the firm

Use the firm’s licensed citator for the dated treatment check. Workbench prepares searches, organizes the results and records counsel’s decision. This workflow works with your existing Lexis login at work.

  1. Take the citation, precise proposition, pinpoint and forum into Lexis. Open the case and select Shepardize.
  2. Inspect direct history and relevant adverse citing decisions. Open the decisions behind the signals. Identify the passage that changes your proposition, or document the scope of the check.
  3. Record the reviewer, date, later decision, pinpoint and effect on the draft. Bring authorized notes and independently obtained public texts back to the matter chat. Keep credentials and licensed reports in the firm environment.
Use case-treatment-check with my dated verification notes and these public opinions. Show the sentence we planned to file, the later court’s limiting language, the proposed revision and anything I still need to check in Lexis.

Check the handoff. A completed row identifies who checked this particular proposition and when. An outstanding search stays open. Blank research return sheet · Workbench treatment assignment · Lexis guide to Shepard’s.
