# Open-source tools for a U.S. business litigator

Start with **LegalQuants Litigation** for a second pass on a brief and **CourtListener** when the assignment needs public authority. Together they cover reviewing supplied material and retrieving new material. Keep Shepard’s and the attorney’s treatment judgment in the firm’s licensed Lexis environment.

Repository counts below are from GitHub on November 8, 2026. Stars and forks describe repository interest. Public installation counts and independent attorney accuracy scores were unavailable for this comparison. Practitioner authorship, tests, and an accountable maintainer are additional selection criteria.

**A ten-minute comparison:** Give two candidate workflows the same public brief excerpt and opinion. Seed a real citation supporting a narrower proposition, a quotation omitting a qualification, and a missing authority. Count which errors each catches, which pinpoints survive inspection, which false alarms it adds and how long counsel spends correcting the result. Keep the tool that reduces total review time on the work you actually do.

## Using Anthropic’s original plugins in Codex

On November 9, 2026, Codex CLI 0.166.0 accepted Anthropic’s unmodified Claude-format marketplace and plugin manifests for both repositories. We tested the linked revisions from GitHub and from unmodified local checkouts in temporary profiles. The Codex app server then listed 19 enabled skills from `litigation-legal@claude-for-legal` v1.0.2 and nine from `legal@knowledge-work-plugins` v1.3.0. The community port also installed with nineteen litigation skills. **Two fictional work samples.** In separate signed-in, read-only Codex turns we supplied the installed skill text and its bundled reference directly. The civil claim chart distinguished an 80-minute response from a 25-minute response after priority escalation, and left timely written notice unproved. Contract review spotted a price notice that could arrive 20 days after the renewal opt-out deadline, calculated a $3,600 maximum increase, and routed the exception to the approver in the supplied playbook. We tested native skill discovery separately. These samples used supplied text with no connected services. ## Natural-language selection in Codex

Workbench 1.3.2 adds a relationship check to deposition drafting. In a fresh same-model replay, the revised grading guidance caught the original two search/email timing assumptions with both graders. The old guidance caught only the more explicit one with each. On a new record both guides already detected every seeded error. One of three old-skill drafts and none of three revised-skill drafts contained an unsupported relationship in a conditional branch. [Frozen record, controls, scores and all six outlines](https://scarson.github.io/litigation-workbench/deposition-timing.html).

For a short fact-witness examination, keep LegalQuants as the everyday starting point and use revised Workbench for a separate outline when event order or witness recollection matters. In a masked comparison on four fresh disputes, LegalQuants averaged 9.81/10, revised Workbench 9.75, Claude for Legal 9.56 and the old Workbench control 9.44. The revised skill outscored its predecessor in five of eight paired runs and tied in three. A separate source audit still found consequential premises to repair in six of eight LegalQuants drafts and five of eight revised Workbench drafts. These are repeated model judgments on fictional individual witnesses. [Four-record comparison, source packets, anonymous drafts and grades](https://scarson.github.io/litigation-workbench/deposition-field-test.html).

The original four-question exercise remains available to try blind. Both graders gave full source-fidelity credit to all three outlines and missed a same-day timing assumption in two. ## What to borrow for a recurring assignment

**When preparing to defend a position**, Jeffrey Snover’s AI Rosetta Stone demonstrates how a strong objection can change a proposed term. Workbench borrows the sequence of writing, challenging and revising. It uses counsel’s supplied record and preserves the reviewer’s objections beside the drafter’s decisions. It runs in the approved matter chat and requires no connection to the Rosetta site. [Snover’s worked debate](https://www.jsnover.com/blog/2026/09/22/steelmaning-ai-policy-with-the-ai-rosetta-stone/) · [MIT-licensed source](https://github.com/AI-Rosetta-Stone/ai-triad-research) · [Workbench example](https://scarson.github.io/litigation-workbench/#argument-review)

**When the question is whether a case is still good law**, start in the firm’s Shepard’s service. Review the direct history and read material citing decisions for the precise point. Workbench can examine the public opinions, recommend a change to the draft and track counsel’s dated review. For an in-chat treatment search, Descrybe Legal Engine offers case-status and citing-case tools through a separate commercial subscription. Its treatment signals are leads for reading the opinions. The base Workbench workflow works without that subscription. [Workbench assignment](https://scarson.github.io/litigation-workbench/#good-law) · [Shepard’s](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-us/products/lexis/shepards.page) · [Descrybe tools and setup](https://descrybe.com/chatgpt) · [Descrybe access and coverage](https://descrybe.com/faq)

These comparisons use the publishers’ source, documentation and GitHub metadata. The worked examples on the setup page exercise Litigation Workbench. We also ran Eyecite on the sample brief text. The other third-party products received a source review. Start with CourtListener for public retrieval and LegalQuants for a second reader. Descrybe is optional for in-chat treatment research.

## How to judge the result on your own matter

**One check you can reproduce.** We ran Eyecite 2.7.8 on the two flawed sentences in the practice file. It extracted `477 U.S. 317` and `Id.` and grouped both under one reference. Reading the opinion then exposed two substantive errors at a perfectly real citation. For a full filing, LegalQuants already supplies a paragraph-by-paragraph reviewer, an HTML report and tests for coverage and incomplete results. Give it the full set of authorized primary sources. Use our brief audit when the immediate need is a prioritized correction and a defensible rewrite. [Eyecite release](https://pypi.org/project/eyecite/2.7.8/) · [LegalQuants review workflow](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/blob/fa5a6681dc3cc9a08fa9ed48a5fd213057edafa0/plugins/legalquants-litigation/skills/cite-check/SKILL.md) · [Upstream tests](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/tree/fa5a6681dc3cc9a08fa9ed48a5fd213057edafa0/packages/skill-tests/tests/cite-check) · [Read the opinion at 323](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep477/usrep477317/usrep477317.pdf#page=7)

| Dimension | A check the lawyer can do in the hour |

| Record and authority | Open the original at the first outcome-changing pinpoint. Check who spoke, the limiting language and contrary material. For law, identify who checked current treatment and when. |

| Confidentiality and portability | Use the account and tools the firm permits for the records. Send a bare public citation to an external lookup when that suffices. Save the reviewed artifact where the firm keeps the matter. The same assignment should start in a fresh matter without importing old facts. |

The U.S. LexisNexis general terms distinguish conditional incidental use of materials from third-party agent access.
